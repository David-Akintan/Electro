# Design direction: Plus Electro

Subject: generator supply, service and soundproofing for Nigerian businesses. Look is taken from the company's own photos.

- Colors: ink #0E1116 (text, dark sections), steel #1D4E9F (SDMO blue, links on light), volt #F26B1D (probe orange: buttons and rules only, always with ink text, never as text on white), concrete #E7E9EC (panels), paper #F6F7F8 (page).
- Type: system sans stack. Headlines 600 weight, tight tracking; body 400, max 65 characters wide.
- Layout: full-bleed photography like tesla.com, but text is left-aligned on a bottom-left gradient. Services are grouped in a spec-sheet list with hairline rows.
- Rules: no numbered lists, no pills, no gradients as decoration, one orange accent per view, motion only on button hover.
